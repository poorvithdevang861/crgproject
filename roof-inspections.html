Roof inspection FAQs.

Everything you need to know about our free diagnostic inspections.

Is the inspection genuinely free?

Yes. There is zero cost, zero call-out fee, and zero obligation to proceed with any repair or replacement work.

Do I need to be home for the inspection?

Not if we have safe perimeter access to set up our ladders. If you have interior ceiling water stains, having someone home allows us to inspect inside as well.

Can this photographic report be used for insurance claims?

Yes. If storm damage or hail impact is detected, our photos, notes, and scopes are formatted specifically to meet insurer lodgement requirements.
