Free Roof Inspections.

Know exactly where your roof stands before spending a single dollar. Clear photo reports within 48 hours.

What We Check

A clear diagnostic of your roof’s health.

Our licensed tradespeople conduct a safe, thorough photographic check across every critical area:

  • Surfaces: Hail strikes, rust spots, coating wear, and loose fixings
  • Flashings: Penetrations, skylights, vent pipes, and wall junctions
  • Valleys & Gutters: Ponding water, debris dams, and drainage fall
  • Ridge Capping: Cracked mortar bedding and wind uplift movement

Common Questions

Good to know.

Is the inspection genuinely free?

Yes. There is zero cost, zero call-out fee, and zero obligation to proceed with any work.

Do I need to be home?

Not if we have safe perimeter access to set up our ladder. If you have interior ceiling stains, having someone home allows us to inspect inside as well.

Can this report be used for insurance?

Yes. If storm damage is identified, our photos and notes are formatted to support your claim lodgement.
