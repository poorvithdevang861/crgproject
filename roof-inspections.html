What We Check

A complete diagnostic of your roof’s integrity.

Our licensed tradespeople conduct an extensive visual and photographic inspection across every critical junction of your roofing system:

  • Metal & Tile Surfaces: Micro-dents, hail strikes, surface rust, coating breakdown, and loose or missing fixings.
  • Flashings & Penetrations: Chimneys, skylights, vent pipes, and wall flashings where 80% of leaks originate.
  • Valley Irons & Gutters: Internal rust, debris accumulation, ponding water, and inadequate drainage fall.
  • Ridge Capping: Cracked mortar bedding, displaced pointing, and storm wind uplift damage.

Frequently Asked

Inspection Questions.

Everything you need to know about the inspection process.

Do I need to be home during the inspection?

As long as our licensed inspector has safe perimeter access around your property to set up a ladder, you do not necessarily need to be home. However, if you suspect active interior ceiling leaks, having someone present allows us to inspect internal watermarks as well.

What will the photographic report include?

You will receive clear digital photographs of every section of your roof, documenting overall condition, flashing seals, valleys, gutters, and any specific hail or rust damage found, accompanied by straightforward recommendations.

Can this report be used for an insurance claim?

Yes. If severe storm or hail damage is identified, our inspection photos and notes are formatted to support your initial claim lodgement, and our partner Claims Approved can assist you with insurer documentation.
