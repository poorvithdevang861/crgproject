Roof replacement FAQs.

Common questions about Colorbond profiles, timelines, and guarantees.

How long does a residential roof replacement take?

A standard single-storey residential replacement typically takes 2 to 4 working days, depending on pitch, structural complexity, and weather conditions. Your roof is never left unprotected overnight.

What Colorbond® colours can I choose from?

You can choose from the entire genuine Colorbond® palette—including favorites like Monument®, Surfmist®, Basalt®, Dune®, and Windspray®. Physical color swatches are provided during consultation.

Can I convert an old concrete tile roof to Colorbond metal?

Yes. Tile-to-Colorbond conversions are one of our core specialties. Colorbond is significantly lighter than brittle concrete tiles, reducing structural load while eliminating cracked tiles and re-pointing costs permanently.
