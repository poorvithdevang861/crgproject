Frequently Asked Roofing Questions.

Everything you need to know about our free inspections, storm damage assessments, Colorbond® steel, and insurance claim advocacy.

Free Roof Inspections
Is the initial roof inspection genuinely 100% free?

Yes. Civic Restoration Group provides completely free roof inspections for Queensland property owners. There are zero call-out charges, zero hidden fees, and zero obligation to proceed with any repairs or restorations afterward.

What will the photographic report show me?

Within 48 hours of our visit, you receive a clear digital report containing high-resolution photos of your roof surfaces, flashings, valley irons, gutters, and ridge capping. Each flaw is explained in plain English with realistic, honest recommendations.

Do I have to be home during the inspection?

No, as long as our licensed inspector has safe perimeter access around your property to position a ladder. If you are experiencing interior ceiling leaks, however, having someone present allows us to inspect internal watermarks as well.

Storm & Hail Damage
How can I tell if my roof has hail damage from the ground?

Hail damage is rarely visible from ground level. Look for secondary signs such as dented metal gutters, battered downpipes, hail marks on air conditioning condenser units, or chipped concrete tile corners. If severe hail struck your suburb, an inspection is recommended.

Can hail damage cause problems even if my ceiling isn't leaking?

Yes. Hail strikes bruise the protective zinc-aluminium coating on steel roofing, which accelerates corrosion and rust. On tile roofs, hail fractures mortar bedding and creates hairline cracks that fail during the next heavy storm season.

Colorbond® Replacement
Do you install genuine BlueScope Colorbond® steel?

Yes, exclusively. We install 100% genuine Australian BlueScope Colorbond® steel engineered specifically for Queensland’s harsh sub-tropical heat, severe storms, and coastal salt environments. We never use cheap unbranded imported sheeting.

How long does a full roof replacement take?

Most residential replacements are completed within 2 to 4 working days. We work in controlled stages and monitor weather radars carefully so your home is always fully watertight and protected throughout the installation.

Do you repair roofs or only replace them?

Both. If your roof only requires targeted maintenance—such as re-pointing ridge caps, resealing a vent flashing, or replacing a rusted valley iron—that is exactly what we recommend. We only suggest replacement when damage is widespread or repairs are uneconomical.

Insurance Claim Support
How do you help with insurance claims?

We provide the detailed photographic evidence and itemised scope of works that insurers require. Together with our independent partner, Claims Approved, we assist you through claim lodgement and assessor meetings to ensure damage is fairly recognized.

What areas of Queensland do you service?

We service properties across South East Queensland (Brisbane, Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, Ipswich, Logan, Moreton Bay, Redlands) as well as the Darling Downs (Toowoomba) and regional Queensland centers.
