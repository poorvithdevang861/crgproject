A Trade Alternative to High-Pressure Roofing.

For too long, roofing has been burdened by aggressive commission-driven sales tactics and inflated quotes. Homeowners are frequently told they need costly full replacements without ever seeing clear evidence of damage.

We created Civic Restoration Group to provide an honest alternative: a trade-first company where qualified inspectors climb up safely, document every elevation with date-stamped macro photographs, and explain findings in plain English.

If your roof is structurally sound and only needs a small seal or routine maintenance, that is exactly what our report states. If severe storm damage or rust warrants replacement, you receive an itemised scope formatted for insurance or fixed-price delivery.

The CRG Code of Practice

  • No High-Pressure Sales: You only deal with qualified trade professionals, never commission-driven salesmen.
  • Date-Stamped Photo Proof: Clear macro and wide-angle photos of all elevations delivered within 48 hours.
  • 100% Genuine BlueScope Steel: Authentic Colorbond® steel backed by BHP/BlueScope statutory warranties.
  • Claims Approved Advocacy: Independent claim guidance and liaison support when dealing with insurers.
