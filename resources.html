How to spot silent hail damage from the ground.

Hail impacts often damage protective anti-corrosion coatings without causing immediate leaks. Watch for these tell-tale signs:

  • Dented metal gutters, downpipes, or external air conditioning condensers
  • Displaced ridge capping pointing and cracked concrete tile edges
  • Interior ceiling water spots that appear weeks after heavy rain

Guide 03 2 min read

Roof repair vs. full replacement: Which makes sense?

Isolated issues can often be patched. Widespread corrosion or multi-pitch storm damage usually warrants full re-roofing:

  • Repairs suit: Single damaged flashings, isolated leaks, or minor gutter replacements.
  • Replacement suits: Brittle aging tiles, widespread hail dents, or rusted iron sheets over 25 years old.
  • Energy upgrade: Colorbond® with Anticon® thermal blanket significantly cuts summer cooling bills.

How to avoid having your roof claim unfairly denied.

Insurers frequently reject claims by calling sudden storm impacts "wear and tear". Protect yourself with these tips:

  • Record the exact date and time of the severe weather event
  • Obtain independent photographic evidence before the insurer’s assessor visits
  • Engage specialised claim advocacy with our partner Claims Approved
