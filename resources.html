What happens during a professional roof inspection?

Climbing onto a roof is dangerous. Here is what licensed trades check to give you peace of mind without sales pressure:

  • Visual and photographic inspection of metal profiles, fasteners, and tile mortar.
  • Check of high-risk penetration points (valleys, skylights, vent flashings).
  • Clear 48-hour photographic summary with honest recommendations.

How to spot silent hail damage from ground level.

Hail impacts often damage protective anti-corrosion coatings without causing immediate interior leaks:

  • Look for dented metal gutters, downpipes, or external AC condenser units.
  • Check for displaced ridge mortar or cracked concrete tile corners.
  • Watch for ceiling moisture stains that emerge weeks after severe rain.

Guide 03 2 min read

Roof repair vs. replacement: Which makes sense?

Isolated issues can usually be patched, while widespread aging often warrants full replacement:

  • Repairs suit: Single loose flashings, localized leaks, or minor gutter sections.
  • Replacement suits: Brittle crumbling tiles, extensive hail dents, or rusted iron over 25 years old.
  • Energy upgrade: Colorbond® with a 55mm thermal blanket dramatically reduces cooling costs.

How to avoid unfair insurance claim denials.

Insurers frequently dispute storm damage by attributing it to pre-existing wear and tear:

  • Record the exact date and approximate time of the weather event.
  • Obtain independent photographic evidence before the insurer's assessor arrives.
  • Partner with independent advocacy specialists like Claims Approved.
