How to spot hail or storm damage.

Storm damage isn’t always visible from the ground. Watch for these signs.

  • Water stains, leaks, ceiling marks or mould
  • Dents, impact marks or damaged coatings on metal roofs
  • Cracked tiles, loose ridge capping or overflowing gutters
Read the full guide

Many owners first notice secondary signs: water staining, leaks, ceiling marks, mould, rust around fixings, loose ridge capping or gutters that no longer drain.

On metal roofs, hail can leave dents and damaged coatings that later lead to rust. On tiled roofs, look for cracked or chipped tiles, broken ridge capping and movement around valleys and penetrations.

If you notice any of these after a storm, book an inspection before the problem spreads inside.

Guide 031 min read

Repair or replace your roof?

Small, isolated issues can usually be repaired. Widespread damage often can’t.

  • Repairs suit small leaks, flashings or a section of ridge capping
  • Damage across several roof areas may need replacement
  • Roof age, coatings, corrosion and past repairs all matter
Read the full guide

A repair makes sense when the problem is isolated: a small leak, a damaged flashing, a short run of ridge capping or a routine maintenance issue.

Hail and storm damage is often different. If impact damage covers several roof faces, patching one spot won’t fix the underlying problem. Age, coating condition, existing rust and earlier repairs all come into it.

When damage is widespread, replacement is usually the more reliable long-term answer, stopping repeat leaks, rust and maintenance costs.
