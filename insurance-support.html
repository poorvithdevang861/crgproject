The Claim Advantage

Why independent documentation matters.

Insurance assessors work for insurance companies. Having an independent assessment ensures damage is fairly evaluated.

Insurers often categorise sudden hail or storm impact as gradual age deterioration. High-resolution photos clearly delineate impact marks.

Common Questions

Insurance claim FAQs.

Can I choose my own roofing contractor for insurance repairs?

Under Australian insurance law and the General Insurance Code of Practice, policyholders generally have the right to request cash settlements or choose their own qualified, licensed builder (such as Civic Restoration Group) rather than accepting an insurer’s preferred contractor.

Who are Claims Approved?

Claims Approved is an independent claims advocacy partner. They assist Queensland property owners with administrative, photographic, and technical paperwork when liaising with insurance companies.

What if my insurer has already partially denied my claim?

Many initial claim denials occur because the insurer’s initial report lacked clear storm impact evidence. We can perform an independent secondary inspection to document the full damage for review.
