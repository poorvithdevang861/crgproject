What to do after severe weather.

Four key actions to protect your property and ensure smooth insurance claims processing.

1. Stay Off the Roof

Wet sheeting and loosened tiles are hazardous. Inspect from ground level and note any damaged gutters or AC units.

2. Record Storm Date

Insurers require the exact weather event date and approximate time of heavy winds, hail, or rainfall.

3. Request Photo Inspection

Our licensed team climbs up safely to capture date-stamped, close-up photos of all roof elevations.

4. File With Solid Proof

Submitting an objective photo report accelerates claim assessment and prevents unfair "wear-and-tear" denials.

Storm & hail damage FAQs.

Common questions about hail strikes, leak detection, and claim timing.

My roof isn’t leaking. Could it still be storm damaged?

Yes. Hail impacts often compromise the protective coating on metal sheets or crack tile bedding. While leaks may not appear immediately, corrosion and water ingress develop over following months.

How long do I have to lodge an insurance claim?

Most Australian insurers require claims to be lodged within 12 to 24 months of the storm event. Prompt photographic evidence ensures causation is clearly established.

Can you assist with insurance claims?

Yes. Together with our independent partner, Claims Approved, we provide the scope of works, photographic evidence, and claim guidance needed for a smooth review.
