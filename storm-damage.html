Common Questions

Good to know.

How do I know if my roof has hail damage?

Look for dents in metal roofing, cracked tiles, damaged ridge capping, leaks, ceiling stains or rust around fixings.

Can a roof be damaged even if I cannot see leaks?

Yes. Some damage weakens coatings or creates small failure points that only cause problems later.

Do you repair roofs or replace them?

Both. Small isolated issues may only need repair, while widespread damage may need a full replacement.
