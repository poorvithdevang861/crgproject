Post-Storm Diagnostic

Hail damage is rarely visible from the ground.

Queensland hailstorms often leave subtle micro-dents that bruise steel protective coatings or dislodge tile bedding without immediate leaks. Over weeks of rain, these points corrode and cause costly internal ceiling damage.

  • Photographic Strike Mapping: We record impact diameter and strike density across all roof pitches.
  • Flashing & Valley Integrity: Wind-lifted flashings and dented valleys that pool water are thoroughly checked.
  • Repair vs. Replacement Analysis: Honest guidance on whether localised sheet repair or full restoration is warranted.
  • Insurer-Ready Formatting: Clear documentation formatted specifically for insurance claims assessors.

Immediate Steps

What to do after a severe weather event.

Protecting your property value and insurance claim integrity starts with these key steps.

1. Do Not Climb Onto the Roof

Wet metal sheeting and cracked tiles are exceptionally hazardous. Stay on the ground and look for secondary signs like dented outdoor AC units or gutter marks.

2. Record the Storm Date

Insurers require the exact weather event date and time. Keep notes of severe wind gusts, hail size, or heavy rainfall duration.

3. Book a Free Photo Inspection

Our licensed inspectors will photograph all exterior damage and provide a comprehensive report within 48 hours for your records.

4. File Your Claim With Evidence

Submitting our itemised photo report drastically speeds up insurer approval and prevents claims being mistakenly classified as wear and tear.

Common Questions

Storm & Hail Damage FAQs.

My roof isn’t leaking. Could it still be storm damaged?

Yes. Hail impacts often compromise the protective zinc-aluminium coating on metal sheets or cause hairline cracks in concrete tiles. While water might not enter immediately, rust and leaks will typically emerge during subsequent heavy weather.

How long do I have to lodge a claim after a storm?

Most Australian insurers require claims to be lodged within a reasonable timeframe (typically 12 to 24 months, depending on the policy). Prompt photographic inspection ensures direct causation to the weather event is indisputable.

Can you assist with insurance claims?

Yes. Together with our independent partner, Claims Approved, we provide the detailed scope of works, photographic evidence, and claim guidance needed to streamline the entire process.
